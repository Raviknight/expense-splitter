Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Splitab is an app for splitting shared expenses with other people. This page explains exactly what it stores, who else can see it, which other companies are involved, and how to get your data removed. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

The short version. Splitab stores the expenses you enter and the account you sign in with. There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is sold to anyone. The app never connects to your bank and never moves money. If you scan a receipt, that image is sent to an AI provider to read it — that is the one place your content leaves our systems, and it is described in detail below.

Who is responsible

Splitab is run by an individual developer based in India. For any question about your data, or to have it deleted, write to hello@splitab.app. That address is also the grievance contact for the purposes of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What is stored

Your account

  • Your email address — needed to sign you in and to let people invite you.
  • A display name you choose.
  • An optional profile photo, if you upload one.
  • Your currency preference, theme, and email-notification settings.

If you sign in with Google, Google passes us your email address and name. We do not receive your Google password, and we cannot see anything else in your Google account.

What you enter in the app

  • Groups you create, and their names.
  • People in those groups. This includes names you type in for people who do not have an account (the app calls them ghost members). Please only add names other people would be comfortable with.
  • Expenses: description, amount, date, category, who paid, how it is split, and any note.
  • Settlements: who paid whom, how much, when, and any note.
  • Your "how to pay me" note, if you write one — for example a UPI ID or "cash is fine". This is free text that you choose to write; do not put anything in it you would not want your group to see.
  • Connection requests between you and other users, and email invitations you send.
  • A record of who deleted an expense, kept so that a group can see why a shared balance changed.
  • Category corrections you make, so the app learns your preferences.

Receipts you choose to scan

If you use the scanning feature, the image or PDF you pick is sent to an AI provider, which reads it and returns the amounts it found. See Receipt scanning below — this is the most important section on this page.

Technical

  • A count of how many receipts you have scanned this month, to apply usage limits.
  • Standard server logs kept by our hosting providers, which include IP addresses.

What is not collected

  • No analytics or tracking of any kind. There is no Google Analytics, no tracking pixel, no advertising network, and no third-party script that follows you.
  • No advertising, and no sale or sharing of your data for anyone else's marketing.
  • No bank or card connection. Splitab never links to a bank account and never sees your card details. It records what you tell it, nothing more.
  • No location tracking, no contacts access, no microphone or camera access except the file you deliberately choose when scanning a receipt.

Receipt scanning — read this one

When you scan a receipt or statement, the file you chose is sent to OpenRouter, which passes it to an AI model to read. If OpenRouter is unavailable, Groq is used instead. The AI returns the merchant, amounts and dates it found, and those become rows you can edit before saving.

This means the image leaves our systems. Two consequences worth understanding:

  • Everything visible in the photo is sent, not just the total — including anything else caught in the frame.
  • How long those providers retain it, and whether they use it further, is governed by their policies, not ours.

Scanning is entirely optional. Every expense can be typed in by hand or imported from a CSV file, and neither of those sends anything to an AI provider. If you would rather no image ever left the app, do not use the scan feature.

Who else can see your data

Other people using Splitab

  • Everyone in a group sees that group's contents — its members, expenses, amounts, notes and settlements. That is the point of a shared group.
  • People you are connected with can see your display name, profile photo, and your "how to pay me" note.
  • If you decline a connection request, that person loses access to your profile.
  • Deleting a shared expense records your name against that deletion for the group.
Profile photos are public. Uploaded photos are stored in a public bucket, which means anyone who has the direct link to the image file can view it, whether or not they use Splitab. Only upload a photo you are comfortable with on that basis.

Legal requests

Data may be disclosed if required by law.

What is stored on your own device

Splitab keeps some things in your browser's local storage so the app opens quickly and works offline. These stay on your device and are cleared when you sign out or clear your browser data. No cookies are set for tracking.

  • slitab.snapshot.<id> — a copy of your groups so the app opens without a network.
  • slitab.outbox.<id> — changes made offline, waiting to sync.
  • splitab.profile.<id> — your name and settings, cached.
  • slitab.scandraft.<id> — scanned rows you have not saved yet.
  • slitab.theme, slitab.homesort, slitab.lastseen.*, slitab.pendingInvite — your display preferences and an invitation link you opened before signing in.

Your sign-in session is also stored here, which is what keeps you logged in.

How long data is kept

Your data is kept while your account exists. Delete an expense, settlement or group and it is removed. Ask us to delete your account and your profile, groups you own, and your expenses are removed.

One honest limitation: expenses in a group you share with other people are part of their record too. If you leave, entries showing what you paid or owed may remain visible to that group, because removing them would silently change everyone else's balances. Tell us if this is a problem for you and we will work out the right answer with you.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete most of your data directly in the app — the Summary tab exports a group to CSV or PDF, and your profile is editable at any time.

You may also ask us to:

  • tell you what data we hold about you,
  • correct anything wrong,
  • delete your account and its data,
  • or raise a complaint about how your data has been handled.

Write to hello@splitab.app. We aim to reply within 30 days. If you are in India, you also have the right to escalate to the Data Protection Board if you are not satisfied with our response.

Age

Splitab is not intended for anyone under 18, and accounts should not be created by children. If we learn that an account belongs to a child, we will delete it.

Security

Your data is protected by database rules that restrict every row to the people entitled to see it, and all traffic uses HTTPS. No system is perfect; if you find a security problem, please report it to hello@splitab.app and it will be taken seriously.

Where data is held

The providers listed above operate internationally, so your data may be processed outside your own country.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top will change and a notice will be shown in the app. Continuing to use Splitab after a change means you accept it.
